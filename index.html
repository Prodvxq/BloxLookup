Commands

/robloxuser
Type a username. Shows the avatar, join date, friends, followers, following, and groups.
/game
Paste a game link or ID. Shows players online now, visits, favorites, votes, and the creator.
/group
Paste a group link or ID. Shows the member count, owner, and whether joining is open.

Only public information

BloxLookup reads what anyone can already see on a Roblox page. It can’t see private details like email addresses or Robux balances, and it doesn’t store anything.
